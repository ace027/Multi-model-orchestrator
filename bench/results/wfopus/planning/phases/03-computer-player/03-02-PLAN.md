Rewrite src/ai.js. Required design: (1) parse the view into a flat Uint8Array blocked map once per call; (2) if me and opponent are in different connected regions (BFS from my head's free neighbours does not reach a cell adjacent to the opponent head), play endgame space-filling: choose the move maximizing an estimate of fillable length (e.g. bounded DFS / longest-path search with articulation-point or checkerboard-parity bound) within the work budget; (3) otherwise run a depth-limited simultaneous-move search (alpha-beta over my move then opponent reply, a paranoid minimax is acceptable) with iterative deepening bounded by a fixed node/BFS budget, evaluating leaves with Voronoi territory (cells strictly closer to me minus strictly closer to opponent, computed by a two-source BFS, optionally weighted by edge count / chamber structure); treat both moving into the same cell as a draw scored 0, and a crash as -inf/+inf; (4) filter immediately fatal moves first and keep the existing contracts ('up' for invalid dir, me.dir when boxed in, never reverse). Use only typed arrays and plain loops on the hot path; no allocations proportional to depth inside the inner loop. Size the work budget so the worst case measured over harness positions on 64x48 stays under 10 ms on this machine.
