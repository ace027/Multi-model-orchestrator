  <name>Task 1: Arena generator, bots and frozen baseline</name>
  <files>bench/arena.js, bench/bots.js, bench/baseline-ai.js</files>
  <action>
bench/arena.js: mulberry32(seed) standard implementation. generateArena(seed,{width,height}): rng=mulberry32(seed); pick a style from rng: 'open' (no obstacles, 15% of seeds), 'scatter' (single cells, density uniform in [0.02,0.08] of W*H), 'walls' (4-12 straight segments, random orientation, length 3-15) or 'mixed' (scatter at half density plus 3-8 segments). Clip to the grid, dedupe, and exclude both start cells (P1 (floor(W/4),floor(H/2)), P2 (W-1-floor(W/4),floor(H/2))) and the 4 cells straight ahead of each start. Return [[x,y],...] sorted row-major. bench/bots.js: shared helpers (inBounds, free cell = grid[y][x]==='.', safe moves = non-reverse dirs with free target, BFS area). random-safe: make(seed) uses mulberry32(seed), picks uniformly among safe moves, else current dir. wall-hugger: among safe moves pick the one whose target cell has the most blocked orthogonal neighbours (out-of-grid, '#', '1', '2'), ties prefer current dir, then order up,right,down,left. flood-fill: pick safe move maximising BFS reachable area from the target cell (target counted as blocked-from-start), ties prefer current dir. voronoi: for each safe move, run a two-source BFS from my target cell and the opponent's current head (with the grid as is plus my target marked occupied); score = cells strictly closer to me minus cells strictly closer to the opponent; pick max, ties by flood-fill area then current dir. All bots are pure functions of the view except random-safe's seeded RNG. Export LADDER in that order (weakest to strongest). bench/baseline-ai.js: copy src/ai.js exactly, changing only its engine import to '../src/engine.js'.
