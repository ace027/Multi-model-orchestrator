- Head-on: both target the same cell -> both crash, draw, neither moves, cell stays '.'

- One crashes, the other moves normally and its new cell becomes trail; survivor scores
- Reverse request ignored; up-then-left while heading right yields up; left-then-up yields up
- Ticks after matchWinner change nothing (snapshot identical)
- chooseMove with all neighbours blocked returns a legal non-reverse string
